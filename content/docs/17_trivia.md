### Did you know?
- **Pong** was one of Atari's earliest major arcade hits. **Tetris** (1984) = falling **tetromino** pieces.
- **Gabe Newell** worked at **Microsoft** before co-founding Valve. Valve's first major game = **Half-Life**; later famous for **Steam**.
- **Steam** is *not* a game: it is a digital distribution and services platform.
- **Roblox** was envisioned as a platform where users create and share experiences, not just one conventional game.

## 5. Apple, Microsoft & Google quick facts
### Apple
- Co-founders **Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, Ronald Wayne** (1976). **Wozniak** designed the **Apple I** (first major PC), then the **Apple II**.
- **Apple bought NeXT** (1996–97), which brought **Steve Jobs** back.
- **iPhone** introduced in **2007** (by Jobs) and popularized the modern smartphone era. iPhone runs **iOS**; Macs run **macOS**.
### Microsoft
- Gates & **Paul Allen** (1975); early stylized name **Micro-Soft**. **Windows** (desktop OS), **Xbox** (vs PlayStation), **.NET**, **Word** (documents), **Excel** (spreadsheets), **PowerPoint** (presentations), **Teams** (team chat/collaboration).
- **Satya Nadella** succeeded **Steve Ballmer** as CEO (2014).
### Google
- **Larry Page & Sergey Brin** started it at **Stanford** as **BackRub**. Owns **YouTube** and **Android**; parent company **Alphabet**; browser **Chrome**.

## 6. Internet, social media & tech firsts
- **Tim Berners-Lee** invented the **World Wide Web** (WWW = World Wide Web). The first website (1991) was about the **WWW project itself**. **ARPANET** was an important precursor to the Internet.
- **Internet ≠ Web:** nobody "invented the Internet" alone; it grew from many researchers and institutions (**Vint Cerf & Bob Kahn** for TCP/IP). The Web is one service running on it.
- **Facebook:** Zuckerberg + Harvard classmates. **Twitter → X** in **2023**. **Instagram:** Systrom & Krieger. **YouTube:** Hurley, Chen, Karim; first video **"Me at the zoo"** by **Jawed Karim**. **Wikipedia:** Wales & Sanger.
- **Ada Lovelace** wrote an early published algorithm for a machine. **Alan Turing** laid foundations of theoretical CS and AI.
- **Linux** (**Linus Torvalds**, first released **1991**), **Git** (also Torvalds), **GitHub** = most-used platform for hosting/collaborating on Git repositories. **Open-source software** = source code available under defined license terms.

### More firsts & facts
- First computer bug: moth, Harvard Mark II, 1947 (Grace Hopper's team). First webcam: Cambridge "Trojan Room coffee pot" (1991). First website: info.cern.ch (1991). First tweet: Jack Dorsey 2006. First domain: symbolics.com (1985). First 1 GB HDD: IBM 3380 (1980, ~refrigerator size). First SMS: "Merry Christmas" (1992). First iPhone June 29, 2007. First video on YouTube: 2005. First hard drive: IBM RAMAC (1956; 5 MB). First mouse: Douglas Engelbart (1964, wooden). First smartphone: IBM Simon (1994). First GUI commercial: Xerox Star (1981). First spreadsheet: VisiCalc (1979). First word processor: WordStar. First browser: WorldWideWeb (1990). First search engine: Archie (1990). First e-commerce transaction: 1994. **Y2K** bug (2000). **Y2038** (32-bit Unix time overflow on 19 Jan 2038). **Unix epoch** = 1 Jan 1970. **Easter egg** concept. **CAPTCHA** = Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart. **Emoji** (Shigetaka Kurita, Japan, 1999). **QR code** (Denso Wave, 1994). **Barcode** (1974 first scanned pack of gum). **GPS** (US; 24+ satellites) vs GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (EU), BeiDou (China). **Moore's law**, **Metcalfe's law** (network value ∝ n²), **Wirth's law**, **Murphy's law**, **Zipf's law**, **Goodhart's law**.

## 8. Trick questions
- **Who invented the Internet?** No single inventor. · **Who invented the Web?** Tim Berners-Lee. · **Are the Internet and the Web the same?** **No.**
- **Did Steve Jobs create Apple alone?** No: co-founders Wozniak and Wayne.
- **Did Elon Musk found Tesla?** No: Eberhard & Tarpenning founded it; Musk joined in 2004.
- **Is HTML a programming language?** No: a **markup** language.
- **Does the original creator still own Minecraft?** No: Microsoft bought Mojang in **2014**.
- **Was Tetris made by a major game company?** No: by programmer **Alexey Pajitnov**.
- **Was Roblox just a conventional video game?** Not exactly: a platform for creating and sharing experiences.
- **Is Steam a game?** No: a digital distribution and services platform.

## 10. Philippine IT
- **DICT** (Department of Information and Communications Technology) — created by **RA 10844 (2016)**; Secretary **Henry Aguda** (appointed Mar 2025). **NTC** regulates telecom; **NPC** privacy; **CICC** cybercrime; **BSP** central bank/payments; **DTI** trade/e-commerce; **PhilSys** national ID (RA 11055); **eGov PH Super App**; **PhilSA** (Philippine Space Agency, RA 11363, 2019); **DOST** (science & tech; **PCIEERD**, **ASTI**); **DepEd/CHED/TESDA**.
- **Telcos:** **Smart** (PLDT group), **Globe**, **DITO** (third major telco, 2021; China Telecom partner). **PLDT** founded 1928. **Converge ICT** (fiber), Sky, PLDT Home. **GCash** (operated by Mynt/Globe Fintech Innovations; launched 2004; the Philippines' largest e-wallet), **Maya** (PayMaya, 2015), **GoTyme**, **UnionBank**, **BDO**, **BPI**, **Metrobank**.
- **E-commerce:** **Lazada** (Alibaba), **Shopee** (Sea Ltd), **TikTok Shop**, **Zalora**, **Carousell**; **Grab** (Anthony Tan), **foodpanda**, **Angkas**, **Lalamove**, **Move It**, **JoyRide**.
- **Laws:** DPA RA 10173; Cybercrime RA 10175; E-Commerce RA 8792; Internet Transactions RA 11967; SIM Registration RA 11934; AFASA RA 12010; Free Wi-Fi RA 10929; Konektadong Pinoy RA 12234; PhilSys RA 11055; DICT RA 10844. 
- **Firsts & people:** **Onel de Guzman** (ILOVEYOU, 2000); **Diosdado "Dado" Banatao** (Filipino-American chip pioneer; first single-chip GUI accelerator, S3); **Roberto del Rosario** (Sing-Along System/karaoke inventor); **Gregorio Zara** (first two-way videophone, 1955); **Diwata-1** (first Philippine microsatellite, 2016); first Philippine Internet connection **PHNet (1994)**; PH = "social media capital of the world" (very high time on social); **.ph** domain via dotPH; **PHOPEX** internet exchange. UMak competes yearly in the **IT Olympics** (this is the **14th**).
- **Philippine AI:** National AI Strategy (NAISR) led by DICT; the BPO/IT-BPM sector is a major AI-disruption topic; regional language models such as **SEA-LION** (AI Singapore) cover Filipino.
- **Also in the coach's bank:** Filipino-American **Diosdado "Dado" Banatao** (microprocessor / PC technologies); **ICT sector** = the term for the government's information and communications technology sector; **NTC** = National Telecommunications Commission regulates telecom and broadcast; **DICT** = Department of Information and Communications Technology (ICT development and policy).
